# Tier dial, usage meter, and site list: every word a person sees

Draft for Jose's review, in the same format as `copy/consent-moment.md`: each `## key`
heading is one piece of on-screen text, `>` lines are notes that never reach the screen,
and words in braces are filled in at the moment.

## Tier dial

## dial.title

## dial.lend

## dial.autonomy

## dial.solo.name
Solo

## dial.solo.summary
Your connection, and only yours.

## dial.solo.lend
Nothing. No one else's traffic touches your Mac.

## dial.solo.autonomy
Asks you before every real-world action. When a site blocks it, you see that, plainly.

## dial.neighborly.name
Neighborly

## dial.neighborly.summary
Members carry each other's agents.

## dial.neighborly.lend
Up to {cap} a month, to reviewed sites only, counted the same both ways.

## dial.neighborly.autonomy
Takes routine steps on listed sites without asking. Still asks before spending money.

## dial.coop.name
Co-op

## dial.coop.summary
More sharing, more room to act.

## dial.coop.lend
A higher monthly cap and a broader list of reviewed sites.

## dial.coop.autonomy
Acts on its own within the budget and preferences you set.
