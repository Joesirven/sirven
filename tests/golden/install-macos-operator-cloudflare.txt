     note: dscl, not sysadminctl -addUser: that one wants a password, and this account never has one.
   12. create the service user agent, hidden, not an administrator (to do, recorded not run under --prefix)
       $ dscl . -create /Users/agent
       $ dscl . -create /Users/agent UserShell /bin/zsh
       $ dscl . -create /Users/agent RealName 'sirven agent'
       $ dscl . -create /Users/agent UniqueID 601
       $ dscl . -create /Users/agent PrimaryGroupID 20
       $ dscl . -create /Users/agent NFSHomeDirectory <prefix>/Users/agent
       $ dscl . -create /Users/agent Password '*'
       $ dscl . -create /Users/agent IsHidden 1
       $ dscl . -create /Users/agent GeneratedUID "$(uuidgen)"   # only when the directory service gave none
